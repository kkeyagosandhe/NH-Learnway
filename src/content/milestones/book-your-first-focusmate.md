title: Book your first Focusmate
do: Schedule one session for tomorrow. You just work quietly next to someone else.
why: A person showing up with you beats waiting to feel motivated.
links:
  - label: Focusmate
    url: https://www.focusmate.com
problem: >-
  The hardest part of studying alone is that nothing outside your own head is
  holding you to it. Cancel on yourself and no one notices. That silence is
  exactly where good intentions go to die — you need something to make the
  commitment real and slightly public.
analogy: >-
  It is the library effect, or the gym-buddy effect. You would never scroll your
  phone for an hour while a stranger sits across a small table clearly working —
  it would feel absurd. Focusmate rents you that quiet social pressure on demand:
  a real person on video who says what they will do, then does it beside you.
intuition: >-
  Psychologists call it body-doubling: the mere presence of another focused
  person makes focus contagious and makes bailing feel costly. You are not asking
  them for help. You are borrowing their momentum. Fifty focused minutes with a
  quiet stranger beats two distracted hours alone.
reflect: "When is your session, and what one problem will you open when it starts?"
