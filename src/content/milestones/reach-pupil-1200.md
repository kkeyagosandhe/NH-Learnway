  This is the capstone, and it comes with a warning: the rating is not the point,

  the upsolving, the ladder. Aim at the reps. Let the number follow.

  rings: you cannot draw them on, you can only grow them, one season at a time, and
  then they quietly record every year you kept going. 1200 is a ring.

  prodigy's number, it is a persistent person's number. It sits downstream of
  everything else on this trail: patterns you can recognise, contests that no longer
  rattle you, a ladder you have been climbing. Do not grind the rating; grind the
  habit, upsolve honestly, and the rating rises on its own. And if it takes longer
  than you hoped — there is no deadline. That was the whole point of this trail.
