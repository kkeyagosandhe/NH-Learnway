chapter: c1
title: Set up your accounts
do: >-
  Create NeetCode, LeetCode, Codeforces, AtCoder, and Focusmate accounts, then
  pick a tracker (Habitica or a paper calendar).
why: Remove every excuse now, while it costs you no willpower.
links:
  - label: NeetCode
    url: https://neetcode.io/practice
  - label: Focusmate
    url: https://www.focusmate.com
  - label: Habitica
    url: https://habitica.com
problem: >-
  Motivation is highest right now and it will never be this cheap again. Every
  account you do not have yet is a tiny locked door your future, tired self will
  use as a reason to quit. So spend today's energy buying down tomorrow's
  friction: get all the doors open while opening them costs you nothing.
analogy: >-
  Think of it like laying out your gym clothes the night before. You are not
  working out yet — you are making it so that the version of you who is tired and
  unsure has nothing left to decide. The accounts are your clothes by the door.
