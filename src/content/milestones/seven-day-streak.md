title: 7-day streak
do: Show up seven days. Misses are fine. Just never skip two days in a row.
why: Never miss twice is what keeps a streak alive.
links:
  - label: Never miss twice
    url: https://jamesclear.com/habit-tracker
problem: >-
  Most streaks die not on the missed day, but on the day after. One skip is an
  accident — a late night, a bad mood, life. The danger is the second skip, because
  two in a row is how your brain quietly rewrites the rule from "I do this" to "I
  used to do this." Protect the day after the miss and the streak is almost
  unbreakable.
analogy: >-
  Comedian Jerry Seinfeld's trick was a big wall calendar and a red X on every day
  he wrote jokes. After a few days you have a chain of X's, and the only job left
  is: don't break the chain. The chain is not made of talent or motivation. It is
  made of not-skipping, and a single link is easy to add.
intuition: >-
  The rule "never miss twice" is forgiving by design. It builds in the fact that
  you are human and will have off days, while refusing to let one off day become a
  slide. Aim for showing up, not for a perfect record — a tiny rep on a bad day
  still counts as a link in the chain.
reflect: "What is your plan for the day AFTER you miss? Decide it now, while it is easy."
