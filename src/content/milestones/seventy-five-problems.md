title: 75 problems solved
do: Reach 75 problems total.
why: Around here, patterns start feeling familiar instead of foreign.
problem: >-
  Somewhere in the seventies, a quiet shift happens. Problems stop feeling like
  strangers. You read one and, before you have decided to, a little voice says
  "this is a sliding window" or "that is just a hash map." You are no longer
  solving each problem from scratch — you are recognising which of a dozen shapes
  it is. That recognition is the actual skill interviews test.
analogy: >-
  It is the moment learning to read flips from sounding out letters to seeing
  whole words. A beginner decodes c-a-t painfully, one letter at a time. A reader
  just sees "cat" and moves on. Seventy-five problems is roughly where you stop
  decoding algorithms letter by letter and start reading them as words.
reflect: "Name three problem 'shapes' you can now recognise on sight. That list IS your progress."
