title: 30-day streak
do: Reach thirty days of showing up. Off days allowed. Never twice in a row.
why: By now, opening the editor is closer to automatic than to a fight.
links:
  - label: Never miss twice
    url: https://jamesclear.com/habit-tracker
problem: >-
  Early on, sitting down to practice took a negotiation with yourself every single
  time. Thirty days of showing up quietly ends that negotiation. The act stops
  being a decision you have to win and starts being just something you do, like
  brushing your teeth. That shift — from effort to default — is the real prize here,
  bigger than any single problem you solved along the way.
analogy: >-
  A path worn through grass. The first person to cross leaves no trace. But walk the
  same line day after day and the grass flattens, then wears, until there is an
  obvious trail that your feet follow without you deciding to. Thirty days is about
  where your practice trail becomes the path of least resistance instead of the hard
  way across the field.
intuition: >-
  Forget the myth of "21 days to a habit" — the real number varies a lot and is
  usually longer. What matters is not a magic count but the same rule that got you
  here: never miss twice. Thirty days of that leaves you with something sturdier
  than motivation — a default. Keep the reps small enough that even a terrible day
  can hold one.
reflect: "What feels different about sitting down to practice now versus on day one?"
