chapter: c2
title: 25 problems solved
do: Reach 25 problems total, at whatever pace your brain allows.
why: Proof the habit is real. The number is not the point.
problem: >-
  Twenty-five is not a skill threshold — nobody becomes good at exactly 25. It is
  a proof-of-life for the habit. It is the point where "I am trying to study
  coding" quietly becomes "I study coding," which is a different sentence about a
  different person. You are not chasing the number; you are chasing the evidence.
analogy: >-
  It is a wall going up brick by brick. No single brick looks like much, and you
  cannot point to the one that made it a wall. But keep laying them and one day you
  step back and there is a wall where there used to be a gap. Each solved problem
  is one brick. Twenty-five is just far enough back to see the shape forming.
reflect: "Which of the 25 was the hardest-won, and what did it teach you?"
